How it works

  1. Sign upStudents join with their branch, roll number and room. Staff request a role and the Principal approves it.
  2. Report or rateSend your problem with photos to the Warden, a Teacher or the Principal, or rate teachers anonymously.
  3. Get it fixedStaff update the status and leave a remark, and you see it instantly on your phone.

Three tools, one campus voice

Problem Sender

Hostel, room, device and mess problems go straight to the right people, with live status tracking.

Teacher Appreciation

Anonymous ratings on teaching clarity, punctuality, doubt solving and behaviour. The best teachers rise to the top.

College Rater

Honest public reviews so future students see the real picture before they take admission.
